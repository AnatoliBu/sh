Tier B

useful-after-audit

Browserbase

https://www.browserbase.com/blog/identity

## Scope

Browserbase's 2026 argument for stable, verifiable agent identity rather than one-off stealth,
including its Verified product direction and Web Bot Auth partnerships.

## Why trusted

Primary source for Browserbase's own product direction and design rationale.

## Caveats

This is a vendor position, not an independent standard or proof that identity products are accepted
by every target site. Partnership and product claims should be verified with each relevant party.

## Extracted rules

- For legitimate user-authorized automation, prefer coherent session identity and explicit trust
  mechanisms over endlessly changing fingerprints.
- Track identity/network continuity as first-class session metadata.
- Separate the strategic trend toward verifiable agent identity from current per-site acceptance.
- Benchmark access outcomes; do not assume a vendor identity flag eliminates all challenges.

## Do not use this source for

A universal claim that Web Bot Auth or Browserbase Verified is accepted across the web.
